About THE 3'Ps

Our Story

THE 3'Ps (PetPal Planner) was created for pet lovers who want a simple way to keep their animals fed, groomed and happy. Busy days make it easy to forget a meal or a brushing session, so we built one friendly place to plan it all.

Our Mission

To make daily pet care easy, organised and stress-free, so every pet gets the attention they deserve.
